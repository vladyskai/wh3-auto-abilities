# Smart Ability Autocast: icon brief and concepts (2026-11-21)

Process: https://github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill (the user's guide: brief, research, 8-12 one-line concepts,
build the three strongest in black first on a 256 grid, test at 16/32/64 px and in one colour, show, then stop).
User: "All those designs look like trash, the first one you did was better. it must be 256x256 pixel."

## 1. Brief

- **Name:** Smart Ability Autocast.
- **What it does:** a Total War: WARHAMMER III mod that casts your units' abilities for you at the right moment. Every
  ability button on the unit's wheel gets an M (manual) or A (automatic) badge.
- **Audience:** WARHAMMER III players browsing the Steam Workshop. The image is seen as a tile (about 200-256 px), in
  the launcher's mod list (about 64 px) and in small lists (about 32 px).
- **Adjectives:** automatic, tactical, dependable, unobtrusive, battle-ready.
- **Constraints:** 256 x 256 PNG under 1 MB, on Steam's dark pages. It should evoke the game's real ability wheel (user,
  with a screenshot): a bronze ring with the ability buttons in an arc on its right. The A badge is the mod's signature.
- **Assumption:** no words in the image. The Workshop tile already shows the title next to it, and words do not survive
  64 px.

## 2. The category and its cliches (to avoid)

WARHAMMER III Workshop thumbnails: busy game screenshots, faction emblems, skulls, lord portraits, big bevelled gold title
text, red-and-black grimdark gradients. They blend together on the Workshop page. A clean flat mark will stand out.
Automation cliches: gears, circular "refresh" arrows, play buttons, robots.

## 3. Word map

automatic -> self-turning, autopilot, loop, clockwork | ability -> button, spell, spark, rune | wheel -> ring, arc, orbit,
beads | M/A -> switch, letter A, badge.

## 4. Concepts (one sentence each, scored on clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small size, out of 25)

1. **Wheel** (pictorial): the game's wheel reduced to geometry, a ring with four button discs along its right arc, and
   the ring holds a bold A. 22
2. **Orbit** (abstract): three ability buttons travelling on a circular track that ends in an arrowhead, the wheel that
   turns by itself. 19
3. **Button A**: one ability button with the A badge in its lower band, nothing else. 15 (generic)
4. **Wheel-letter A** (letterform): a letter A whose crossbar is a row of three ability buttons. 20
5. **Geared button**: an ability button with gear teeth for a rim. 13 (automation cliche)
6. **Switch**: a toggle switch whose knob is an ability button set to A. 14 (literal UI widget)
7. **Clock hand**: a wheel with a hand pointing at the next button. 12 (explains, does not identify)
8. **Rune A**: an angular rune-like A inside a button. 14 (fantasy cliche)
9. **Half wheel**: only the right half of the ring with its four buttons, cut sharp, like a crescent of buttons. 17

Built: 1 (Wheel), 2 (Orbit), 4 (Wheel-letter A): the three strongest that are most different (pictorial, abstract,
letterform).

## 5. Palette

Charcoal #16120F (ground), bronze #C8914F (the wheel's metal), badge green #4FAE5A (the A, as in the mod), bone
#F0E6D0 (letters). Three colours plus the ground.
