## 2. The changes (both copies: the live source and the annotated reading copy)

1. **Running and closing** (`tools\round192_patch.py`). `FOURTH.fast_enemy_near` used to accept any moving enemy whose
   unit type runs at 5 m/s or more within 40 m of one of ours, even one riding past or walking. Now it needs:
   - the enemy RUNNING: `is_moving_fast()` exactly true;
   - the enemy CLOSING: `FOURTH.fast_closing` keeps one sample per enemy per poll of its edge gap to our nearest unit;
     the gap must have shrunk by at least `FOURTH.FAST_CLOSING_MPS = 3` m/s over at least `FOURTH.FAST_CLOSING_S = 1` s.
   The run-speed stat (5 m/s) and the 40 m closeness stay. The first answer comes one second after the enemy starts
   running. Every read fails closed. Used by enemy_fast_near_ally (19 entries) and the fast arms of enemy_melee_or_fast,
   enemy_fast_or_melee, enemy_fast_fire_melee, enemy_wave_charge and slow_army (30 workbook rows). TEST-ONLY:
   `ZZ_AUTO_TEST_NO_CLOSING` keeps the older harness worlds on the old rule.
2. **The Moulder rule** (`tools\round192_moulder.py`, `round192_moulder2.py`). The game's flag is named
   `unit_is_moulder_monster`, but its own text (`special_ability_invalid_usage_flags` loc, flag_description) reads "Unit
   is not a Clan Moulder Monster": the target must BE one (checked by the research agent from the game's own text; the
   tiger-warrior flags read the same way). GENERATED `FOURTH.MOULDER_TEXT` (`tools\gen_rule_sets.py` derive_moulder: the
   `moulder_monster` attribute, 28 units: Rat Ogres, Hell Pit Abominations, Brood Horrors, Wolf Rats, Stormfiends, the
   Moulder lords and heroes on those mounts); code M in `gen_usage_keys.TARGET`; `FOURTH.ab_atarget_ok` honours it; the
   card adds "Clan Moulder monster allies only". Remouldered's own "monster" list (which left out plain Rat Ogres, Wolf
   Rats and four Stormfiend kinds) yields to the game's rule.
3. **Grombrindal Has No Fear**: a new entry on melee_or_wavering_aura (aura 55 m, bound 38 m; it starts armed with its
   family). Its key is filed by the game under `source_type = passive`, which the inventory rule had missed.
4. **The five runes removed**: Rune of Breaking, Negation, Slowness, Speed, Oath & Steel (entries and charge-gate rows;
   the generated ANIM / RECHARGE / ATARGET tables regenerated by `tools\round171_regen_tables.py`). Roster: **984 entries
   / 1,037 keys / 141 families**.

## 3. What changes in battle

- The slows and snares on the fast-enemy trigger (Mither, Net of Amyntok, Master Rune of Slowness, Whispering Mist,
  Hunter's Snare ...) fire only on a cavalry-speed enemy that is running AT us. An enemy riding past, walking or
  running away no longer spends them. Cost: about one second of delay after the enemy starts its run.
- Shock Collar and Remouldered wait for a Clan Moulder monster; they no longer try a Skeleton unit the game refuses.
- Grombrindal Has No Fear fires when Grombrindal or a friendly unit within 38 m is in melee or wavering.
- The five runes get no M/A letter at all.
