## 1. The rulings (user, 2026-11-08, both as recommended)

- **Pure missile protection, "When under fire":** Shield of Chaqua (Missile Resistance +40, aura 55 m,
  3 uses), The Golden Eye of Tzeentch (Missile Resistance +40, barrier regeneration), The Lion's Shield
  (Spell and Missile Resistance +40).
- **Mixed protection, "In melee or under fire":** The Blessed Helm of the Oblast (Armour +60, Missile
  Resistance +20, aura 35 m), The Bane Shield (Armour +30, Missile Block +24, aura 35 m, 1 use), Charmed
  Shield (Missile Block +25, Damage Resistance +20), Glacial Defence (Armour +60, Missile Resistance +20,
  Speed -25%).
- **Earlier decisions:** none beyond the 2026-10 in-melee approval of each.

## 2. The change

| Family (NEW) | Abilities | Fires when | Cast |
|---|---|---|---|
| `under_fire` | Shield of Chaqua, The Golden Eye of Tzeentch, The Lion's Shield | the caster is under missile fire; for Shield of Chaqua also any friendly unit within 38 m (70% of 55 m) | the caster |
| `melee_or_under_fire` | The Blessed Helm, The Bane Shield, Charmed Shield, Glacial Defence | the caster is in melee (not wavering) or under fire; for the two auras also any friendly unit within 24 m (70% of 35 m) in melee or under fire | the caster |

**The read:** `battle_unit:is_under_missile_attack()`, the read the enemy picker already uses. Only an
exact true counts; an unreadable or raising read is "not under fire". It has never been seen on the
player's own units in a real battle, so the QA battle is its first field proof. If it never answers
true, the three pure-protection abilities simply never fire (the melee arm of the other four still
works).
