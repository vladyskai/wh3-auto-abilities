## 3. What the mod now does, in plain words

Before an enemy can become the target of one of these 35 abilities, the mod draws a straight line from the caster to
that enemy and asks two questions.

1. **Does any other unit stand across the line?** Every unit on the field counts: ours and the enemy's (hidden enemies
   too, because they physically stand there). Each unit is a rectangle (its frontage from the game's `ordered_width`,
   its depth estimated from its men and the game's spacing table, the same estimate the blast checks use), grown by 1 m
   on every side. A unit blocks when the line passes over its rectangle lower than the unit's top (2.5 m for man-sized
   units, 6 m for the large set) and higher than its feet. Heights come from each unit's live position, so:
   - a caster on a hill can shoot over units standing below it;
   - a flying unit high in the air does not block a line that passes under it.
2. **Does the ground rise above the line?** The mod reads the ground height (`bm:get_terrain_height`) every 4 m along
   the line. The line starts 1.5 m above the caster and ends 1 m above the target. If the ground comes within 0.5 m of
   the line anywhere, the line is blocked.

The line only counts the stretch between the caster's own rectangle and the target's rectangle (the caster and the
target never block themselves).

**A blocked enemy is skipped, not the whole cast.** Every picker of the 7 triggers already asks one shared question of
each candidate ("is this enemy a valid target?"); the line check is now part of that question. So the picker simply moves
on to the next best enemy with a clear line. If none is clear, the ability waits. The last check before the cast asks the
same question again.

**Safety first (the ruling): every doubt blocks.**
- A position that cannot be read: blocked.
- The ground read missing or failing: blocked, and one log line per battle says so:
  `LINE OF FIRE: bm:get_terrain_height() is unreadable - the line-of-sight abilities hold`.
- The ground read is checked first against a unit standing on it (the caster, else the target): the ground under it
  must lie within 3 m of the unit's own height. If not, the read is not trusted and the target is blocked, with one log
  line per battle: `LINE OF FIRE: the ground read is X m off the height of a unit standing on it`.
- The flying read unreadable on both caster and target: blocked.

Every block is logged (once every 10 s per ability, caster, enemy and kind):
`<ability> LINE OF FIRE BLOCKED to <enemy type>: a unit of ours (<type>) stands across the line 75 m out - not a target`,
or `... the ground rises across the line 72 m out (0.8 m above it) - not a target`.

## 4. What it cannot see (stated plainly)

- **Trees, buildings, walls.** I assume `get_terrain_height` returns the bare ground. A forest or a house between the
  caster and the target is not seen.
- **Real unit heights.** The API has no read; 2.5 m and 6 m are my assumptions.
- **The real unit shapes.** The rectangles are the same estimate the blast checks use (the API has no bounding box).
- **Whether the game itself counts enemy units as blocking.** The Wulfhart battle only proved that our own units do. The
  ruling counts both.
- **`get_terrain_height` has never been read in our battles.** If it fails or answers something unexpected, the 35
  abilities stay silent and the log says why (the ruling: better not to fire).

   entry needs the line).
4. The load loop: each entry gets `ability.los` from its keys.
